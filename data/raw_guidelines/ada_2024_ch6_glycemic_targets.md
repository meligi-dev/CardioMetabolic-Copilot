# ADA Standards of Care in Diabetes — 2024
## Chapter 6: Glycemic Goals and Hypoglycemia Management

### 6.1 Assessment of Glycemic Control and Biomarkers
* **Recommendation 6.1:** An **HbA1c test** should be performed at least twice a year in patients meeting treatment goals (and who have stable glycemic control) and quarterly in patients whose therapy has changed or who are not meeting glycemic goals. (Level E)
* **Recommendation 6.2:** For individuals using Continuous Glucose Monitoring (**CGM**), the standardized metrics to evaluate glycemic control include:
  * **Time in Range (TIR, 70–180 mg/dL):** Target >70% of readings for most non-pregnant adults.
  * **Time Below Range (TBR <70 mg/dL - Hypoglycemia):** Target <4% (with <1% <54 mg/dL).
  * **Time Above Range (TAR >180 mg/dL - Hyperglycemia):** Target <25%.
  * In older or high-risk/frail adults, the TIR target is relaxed to >50% and TBR <1% (<70 mg/dL) to prioritize hypoglycemia avoidance. (Level A)

### 6.2 Individualized Glycemic Targets
* **Recommendation 6.3:** An **HbA1c goal of <7.0% (53 mmol/mol)** without significant hypoglycemia is appropriate for many non-pregnant adults. (Level A)
* **Recommendation 6.4:** More stringent HbA1c goals (such as **<6.5%**) may be reasonable for select individuals (short diabetes duration, long life expectancy, no significant CVD) if achieved without significant hypoglycemia. (Level C)
* **Recommendation 6.5:** Less stringent HbA1c goals (such as **<8.0%**) are appropriate for patients with limited life expectancy, extensive comorbid conditions, advanced microvascular or macrovascular complications, or severe hypoglycemia history. (Level B)

### 6.3 Hypoglycemia Classification & Emergency Treatment
* **Recommendation 6.6 (Hypoglycemia Levels):**
  * *Level 1 (Mild):* Blood glucose 54–69 mg/dL. Treat with 15–20g of fast-acting glucose (Rule of 15); recheck in 15 min.
  * *Level 2 (Moderate/Severe biochemical):* Blood glucose <54 mg/dL. Immediate fast-acting carbohydrate administration.
  * *Level 3 (Severe Clinical Hypoglycemia):* Severe cognitive impairment requiring third-party assistance.
* **Recommendation 6.7 (Glucagon Prescribing):** **Glucagon** (nasal powder e.g. Baqsimi 3mg or autoinjector) should be prescribed for all individuals taking insulin or sulfonylureas at high risk for Level 2 or 3 hypoglycemia. (Level E)
