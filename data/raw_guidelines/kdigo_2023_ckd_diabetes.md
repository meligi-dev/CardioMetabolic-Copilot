# KDIGO 2023 Clinical Practice Guideline for Diabetes Management in CKD
## Chapter 1: Comprehensive Care & Pharmacotherapy in CKD

### 1.1 Screening and Risk Stratification
* **Recommendation 1.1:** Screen adults with diabetes for chronic kidney disease at least annually with:
  1. Spot urinary albumin-to-creatinine ratio (**UACR**):
     - Normal to mildly increased: <30 mg/g (A1)
     - Moderately increased (microalbuminuria): 30–300 mg/g (A2)
     - Severely increased (macroalbuminuria): >300 mg/g (A3)
  2. Estimated glomerular filtration rate (**eGFR**):
     - Stage G1: ≥90 mL/min
     - Stage G2: 60–89 mL/min
     - Stage G3a: 45–59 mL/min
     - Stage G3b: 30–44 mL/min
     - Stage G4: 15–29 mL/min (Severe CKD)
     - Stage G5: <15 mL/min (Kidney failure)

### 1.2 Pharmacological Therapy Pillars in Diabetic Kidney Disease
* **Recommendation 1.2 (SGLT2 Inhibitor in CKD):** Treat patients with type 2 diabetes, CKD, and an eGFR ≥20 mL/min/1.73 m² with an SGLT2 inhibitor (Empagliflozin, Dapagliflozin, or Canagliflozin). SGLT2 inhibitors provide primary nephroprotection, slowing the loss of eGFR and reducing ESKD risk. Once initiated, continue even if eGFR falls below 20 mL/min until renal replacement therapy is initiated. (Level 1A)
* **Recommendation 1.3 (Nonsteroidal MRA - Finerenone):** We recommend a nonsteroidal mineralocorticoid receptor antagonist (Finerenone) for patients with type 2 diabetes and eGFR ≥25 mL/min with persistent albuminuria (UACR ≥30 mg/g) despite maximally tolerated ACEi or ARB therapy. Finerenone reduces CKD progression and cardiovascular events. Monitor serum potassium. (Level 1A)
* **Recommendation 1.4 (GLP-1 RA in CKD):** In patients with type 2 diabetes and CKD who do not achieve individualized glycemic targets with metformin and an SGLT2 inhibitor, or who cannot use these drugs, we recommend a long-acting GLP-1 RA (Semaglutide or Dulaglutide) with proven CV and renal outcomes. (Level 1B)
* **Recommendation 1.5 (RAS Blockade):** Initiate an ACE inhibitor or ARB in patients with diabetes, hypertension, and albuminuria (UACR ≥30 mg/g). Titrate to the highest approved dose tolerated. Do NOT combine ACEi and ARB due to hyperkalemia and acute kidney injury risk. (Level 1B)
