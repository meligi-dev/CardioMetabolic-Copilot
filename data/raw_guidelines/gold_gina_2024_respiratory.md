# GOLD 2024 & GINA 2024 Guidelines for Respiratory Medicine
## COPD and Asthma Management in Hospital & Outpatient Settings

### 1. GOLD 2024 Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease (COPD)
* **Recommendation 1.1 (Initial Pharmacotherapy by ABCD/E Groups):**
  * **Group A (Low symptoms, 0–1 moderate exacerbations):** A bronchodilator (short or long-acting SABA or LABA/LAMA).
  * **Group B (High symptoms CAT ≥10, 0–1 moderate exacerbations):** Initial dual bronchodilator therapy: **LABA + LAMA** (e.g., Tiotropium + Olodaterol or Umeclidinium + Vilanterol) is superior to monotherapy. (Class I, Level A)
  * **Group E (High exacerbation risk ≥2 moderate or ≥1 hospitalization):** Initial therapy with **LABA + LAMA**. If blood eosinophils are **≥300 cells/μL**, initiate **Triple Therapy (LABA + LAMA + Inhaled Corticosteroid [ICS])** (e.g., Fluticasone furoate/Umeclidinium/Vilanterol). (Class I, Level A)
* **Recommendation 1.2 (Acute COPD Exacerbation Inpatient Management):**
  * Systemic Corticosteroids: **Prednisone 40 mg orally daily for 5 days** (improves recovery and shortens hospitalization without prolonged taper). (Class I, Level A)
  * Antibiotics: Indicated if all 3 cardinal symptoms (increased dyspnea, increased sputum volume, increased sputum purulence) or requirement for mechanical ventilation (e.g., Azithromycin 500mg daily for 5 days or Amoxicillin/Clavulanate).

### 2. GINA 2024 Asthma Management & Track 1 SMART Therapy
* **Recommendation 2.1 (Anti-inflammatory Reliever Preference - Track 1):** In adults and adolescents with asthma, the preferred reliever across all severity steps (Steps 1 through 5) is **low-dose ICS-Formoterol** (Budesonide/Formoterol 160/4.5 μg as needed for symptom relief). This reduces the risk of severe exacerbations by >60% compared to SABA (Albuterol) alone. (Class I, Level A)
* **Recommendation 2.2 (SABA Monotherapy Warning):** SABA (Albuterol) monotherapy without inhaled corticosteroids is **NO LONGER RECOMMENDED** due to increased risk of asthma-related death and airway remodeling. (Class III: Harm, Level A)
