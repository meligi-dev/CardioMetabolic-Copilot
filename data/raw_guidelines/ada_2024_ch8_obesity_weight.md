# ADA Standards of Care in Diabetes — 2024
## Chapter 8: Obesity and Weight Management for the Prevention and Treatment of Type 2 Diabetes

### 8.1 Pharmacotherapy for Weight Loss in T2D
* **Recommendation 8.1:** In people with type 2 diabetes and overweight or obesity, obesity management should be considered an active treatment goal alongside glycemic control. (Level A)
* **Recommendation 8.2 (Efficacy Tiers for Weight Loss):** When choosing glucose-lowering medications for individuals with type 2 diabetes and overweight/obesity, prioritize agents with proven weight loss efficacy:
  * **Very High Weight Loss Efficacy (≥15% body weight loss):** **Tirzepatide** (dual GIP/GLP-1 receptor agonist, titrated up to 15mg weekly) and **Semaglutide 2.4mg** (Wegovy). (Class I, Level A)
  * **High Weight Loss Efficacy (10–15% body weight loss):** Semaglutide 1.0–2.0mg (Ozempic), **Liraglutide 3.0mg** (Saxenda). (Class I, Level A)
  * **Intermediate Weight Loss Efficacy (5–10% body weight loss):** **Dulaglutide**, SGLT2 inhibitors (Empagliflozin, Dapagliflozin). (Level A)
  * **Weight Neutral:** DPP-4 inhibitors (Sitagliptin, Linagliptin), Metformin.
  * **Avoid Weight Gain Agents:** Insulin, Sulfonylureas, Thiazolidinediones when weight loss is the primary goal.

### 8.2 Metabolic and Bariatric Surgery Criteria
* **Recommendation 8.3:** Metabolic bariatric surgery (Roux-en-Y gastric bypass or Sleeve gastrectomy) should be recommended in individuals with T2D who have:
  * BMI ≥35.0 kg/m² (or ≥32.5 kg/m² in Asian American individuals) regardless of glycemic control.
  * BMI 30.0–34.9 kg/m² (or 27.5–32.4 kg/m² in Asian Americans) if hyperglycemia is inadequately controlled despite optimal medical therapy. (Level A)
