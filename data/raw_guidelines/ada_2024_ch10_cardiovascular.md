# ADA Standards of Care in Diabetes — 2024
## Chapter 10: Cardiovascular Disease and Risk Management

### 10.1 Screening and Risk Assessment
* **Recommendation 10.1:** Blood pressure should be measured at every routine clinical visit. In individuals with diabetes and hypertension, blood pressure should be treated to a target of <130/80 mmHg, if it can be safely attained. (Level B)
* **Recommendation 10.2:** In all patients with diabetes, atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease (ASCVD) risk factors should be assessed at least annually. These risk factors include duration of diabetes, obesity/overweight, hypertension, dyslipidemia, smoking, family history of premature CAD, chronic kidney disease (CKD), and presence of albuminuria. (Level A)

### 10.2 Glucose-Lowering Therapies with Cardiovascular and Renal Benefit
* **Recommendation 10.4 (ASCVD with Diabetes):** In adults with type 2 diabetes and established ASCVD (prior myocardial infarction, stroke, coronary revascularization, carotid disease, or peripheral artery disease), or high risk for ASCVD, the recommended pharmacotherapy includes a **GLP-1 Receptor Agonist** (e.g., Semaglutide, Dulaglutide, Liraglutide) or an **SGLT2 Inhibitor** (e.g., Empagliflozin, Dapagliflozin, Canagliflozin) with proven cardiovascular outcome trial (CVOT) benefit. This is recommended to reduce major adverse cardiovascular events (MACE) independent of baseline HbA1c or individualized HbA1c target. (Class I, Level A)
* **Recommendation 10.5 (Heart Failure with Diabetes):** In adults with type 2 diabetes and established heart failure (either Heart Failure with Reduced Ejection Fraction [HFrEF] or Heart Failure with Preserved Ejection Fraction [HFpEF]), an **SGLT2 inhibitor** with proven benefit in this population (Empagliflozin or Dapagliflozin) is recommended. This therapy reduces the risk of worsening heart failure, cardiovascular death, and all-cause hospitalization, irrespective of baseline HbA1c. (Class I, Level A)
* **Recommendation 10.6 (Combined ASCVD & CKD):** For patients with type 2 diabetes and established chronic kidney disease (eGFR 20–60 mL/min/1.73 m² or UACR >30 mg/g), both an **SGLT2 inhibitor** and a **GLP-1 RA** should be considered in combination to provide additive protection against CKD progression, MACE, and heart failure hospitalization. (Class I, Level A)

### 10.3 Lipid Management in Diabetes & CVD
* **Recommendation 10.10 (Statin Therapy Primary Prevention):** For people with diabetes aged 40–75 years without ASCVD, use moderate-intensity statin therapy in addition to lifestyle therapy. (Level A)
* **Recommendation 10.11 (Statin Therapy Secondary Prevention / High Risk):** For people with diabetes aged 40–75 years with established ASCVD or 10-year ASCVD risk ≥20%, high-intensity statin therapy is recommended to target an LDL cholesterol reduction of ≥50% from baseline and an LDL cholesterol target of <55 mg/dL. (Class I, Level A)
* **Recommendation 10.12 (Add-on Lipid Therapies):** If LDL cholesterol remains ≥55 mg/dL on maximally tolerated high-intensity statin, add **Ezetimibe** (Class I, Level A) or a **PCSK9 inhibitor** (Class I, Level A).
