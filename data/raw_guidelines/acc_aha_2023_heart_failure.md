# ACC/AHA/HFSA Guideline for the Management of Heart Failure
## 2022 Guideline & 2023 Focused Update

### 1. Guideline-Directed Medical Therapy (GDMT) for HFrEF (LVEF ≤ 40%)
* **Recommendation 1.1 (The 4 Pillars of GDMT):** In patients with Heart Failure with Reduced Ejection Fraction (HFrEF, LVEF ≤ 40%), GDMT should consist of four core pharmacological classes titrated to target or maximally tolerated doses:
  1. **ARNI (Sacubitril/Valsartan)** or ACEi/ARB (ARNI preferred first-line, Class I, LOE A).
  2. **Evidence-based Beta-Blocker** (Carvedilol, Metoprolol Succinate extended-release, or Bisoprolol) (Class I, LOE A).
  3. **Mineralocorticoid Receptor Antagonist (MRA)** (Spironolactone or Eplerenone) (Class I, LOE A).
  4. **SGLT2 Inhibitor** (Dapagliflozin or Empagliflozin) (Class I, LOE A).
* **Recommendation 1.2 (SGLT2i Independence of Diabetes):** SGLT2 inhibitors (Empagliflozin 10mg daily or Dapagliflozin 10mg daily) are recommended for all patients with HFrEF to reduce cardiovascular mortality and heart failure hospitalizations, **regardless of whether the patient has diabetes or not**. (Class I, LOE A)

### 2. Management of HFmrEF (LVEF 41–49%) and HFpEF (LVEF ≥ 50%)
* **Recommendation 2.1 (SGLT2i in HFpEF/HFmrEF):** In patients with HFpEF or HFmrEF, SGLT2 inhibitors (Empagliflozin or Dapagliflozin) are recommended to reduce heart failure hospitalizations and cardiovascular mortality. (Class I, LOE A)
* **Recommendation 2.2 (ARNI and MRA in HFpEF):** In patients with HFpEF, MRAs (Spironolactone) and ARNIs (Sacubitril/Valsartan) may be considered, particularly among patients with LVEF at the lower end of the spectrum (LVEF 50–57%). (Class IIb, LOE B)

### 3. Critical Red Flags & Contraindicated Medications in Heart Failure
* **Thiazolidinediones (TZDs - Pioglitazone, Rosiglitazone):** **CONTRAINDICATED** in patients with established heart failure (NYHA Class I–IV). Causes sodium/fluid retention and precipitates acute decompensation. (Class III: Harm, LOE A)
* **Non-Dihydropyridine Calcium Channel Blockers (Diltiazem, Verapamil):** Contraindicated in HFrEF due to negative inotropic effects. (Class III: Harm)
* **NSAIDs (Ibuprofen, Naproxen, Celecoxib):** Avoid in heart failure; induces sodium retention, blunts diuretic efficacy, and exacerbates renal dysfunction. (Class III: Harm)
* **Certain DPP-4 Inhibitors (Saxagliptin, Alogliptin):** Avoid due to increased risk of heart failure hospitalization (SAVOR-TIMI 53 trial). (Class III: Harm)
