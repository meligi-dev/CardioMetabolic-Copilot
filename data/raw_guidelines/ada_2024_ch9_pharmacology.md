# ADA Standards of Care in Diabetes — 2024
## Chapter 9: Pharmacologic Approaches to Glycemic Treatment

### 9.1 First-Line Therapy and Individualized Goal Setting
* **Recommendation 9.1:** Healthy lifestyle behaviors, diabetes self-management education and support (DSMES), social determinants of health (SDOH) assessment, and pharmacotherapy should be initiated at diagnosis of type 2 diabetes. (Level A)
* **Recommendation 9.2:** Pharmacotherapy should be guided by person-centered factors: cardiovascular (ASCVD) status, heart failure status, chronic kidney disease (CKD) status, glycemic efficacy, weight management goals, hypoglycemia risk, and cost. (Level A)

### 9.2 Renal Thresholds and Organ-Specific Cutoffs
* **Metformin:**
  * *eGFR ≥45 mL/min/1.73 m²:* Full standard dose (up to 2000–2550 mg/day).
  * *eGFR 30–44 mL/min/1.73 m²:* Limit maximum dose to 1000 mg/day. Do not initiate new treatment in this range unless carefully monitored.
  * *eGFR <30 mL/min/1.73 m²:* **Contraindicated.** Discontinue immediately due to risk of lactic acidosis.
* **SGLT2 Inhibitors (Empagliflozin, Dapagliflozin, Canagliflozin):**
  * *Initiation Threshold for Glycemic Lowering:* eGFR ≥45 mL/min (glycemic efficacy decreases at lower eGFR).
  * *Initiation Threshold for Cardiorenal & HF Protection:* eGFR down to 20 mL/min/1.73 m². Continue therapy until dialysis or kidney transplantation.
* **GLP-1 Receptor Agonists (Dulaglutide, Semaglutide, Liraglutide):**
  * *Renal Dosing:* No dose adjustment needed across all eGFR ranges (down to eGFR 15 mL/min). Exceptional safety in renal impairment.
* **DPP-4 Inhibitors (Sitagliptin, Linagliptin, Saxagliptin):**
  * Linagliptin: No renal dose adjustment needed (biliary elimination).
  * Sitagliptin: Reduce dose to 50mg if eGFR 30–44; reduce to 25mg if eGFR <30.
  * **Warning:** Saxagliptin and Alogliptin increase heart failure hospitalization risk; avoid in heart failure.
* **Sulfonylureas (Glimepiride, Glipizide, Glyburide):**
  * Glipizide preferred in CKD. Glyburide contraindicated in renal impairment due to severe prolonged hypoglycemia risk.

### 9.3 Weight Management Goals in T2D
* For individuals with type 2 diabetes and overweight/obesity:
  * **Very High Efficacy for Weight Loss:** Tirzepatide (dual GIP/GLP-1 RA) > Semaglutide (GLP-1 RA).
  * **High Efficacy for Weight Loss:** Dulaglutide, Liraglutide.
  * **Intermediate Weight Loss:** SGLT2 inhibitors.
  * **Weight Neutral:** DPP-4 inhibitors, Metformin.
  * **Weight Gain Risk:** Insulin, Sulfonylureas, Thiazolidinediones.
