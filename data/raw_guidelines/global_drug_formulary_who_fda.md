# World Health Organization & FDA Essential Drug Formulary
## Global Hospital Pharmacopeia & Renal Dosing Reference

### 1. Anticoagulation & Antiplatelet Formulary
* **Apixaban (Eliquis):**
  * *Standard Dosing:* 5mg PO BID for NVAF.
  * *Renal/Dose Reduction Criteria (2 of 3 rule):* Reduce to 2.5mg BID if any 2 of: Age ≥80 years, Body weight ≤60 kg, or Serum Creatinine ≥1.5 mg/dL.
  * *Interactions:* Avoid with strong dual inhibitors of CYP3A4 and P-gp (Ketoconazole, Itraconazole).
* **Clopidogrel (Plavix):**
  * *Standard Dosing:* 300–600mg loading dose, then 75mg PO daily.
  * *Black Box Warning:* Reduced antiplatelet efficacy in CYP2C19 poor metabolizers. Avoid concurrent Omeprazole/Esomeprazole (blocks conversion to active metabolite; Pantoprazole is the preferred PPI).

### 2. Critical Antibiotic Renal Dosing
* **Vancomycin:**
  * *Loading Dose:* 20–35 mg/kg IV in severe infection.
  * *Maintenance:* Dose adjusted based on trough levels (target 15–20 mcg/mL in severe sepsis/MRSA pneumonia) and eGFR/creatinine clearance (CrCl).
  * *Toxicity Warning:* Synergistic nephrotoxicity with Piperacillin/Tazobactam (Zosyn) and aminoglycosides.
* **Piperacillin/Tazobactam (Zosyn):**
  * *Standard Dose:* 3.375g–4.5g IV Q6H (or 3.375g over 4 hours extended infusion).
  * *CrCl 20–40 mL/min:* 2.25g Q6H.
  * *CrCl < 20 mL/min:* 2.25g Q8H.

### 3. Gastrointestinal & Acid Suppression
* **Pantoprazole (Protonix):**
  * *Standard Dose:* 40mg PO/IV daily (or 80mg IV bolus followed by 8mg/hr infusion for active peptic ulcer bleeding).
  * *Advantage:* Minimal CYP2C19 inhibition; preferred in patients taking Clopidogrel (Plavix).
