# ACC/AHA & ADA 2023/2024 Clinical Practice Guideline
## Hypertension and Dyslipidemia Management in Cardiovascular Disease

### 1. Blood Pressure Targets & Stepwise Pharmacotherapy
* **Recommendation 1.1 (Target Blood Pressure):** In adults with confirmed hypertension and diabetes or established ASCVD, target blood pressure is **<130/80 mmHg** if safely achievable without symptomatic orthostasis. (Class I, Level A)
* **Recommendation 1.2 (Initial Dual Combination Therapy):** In patients with confirmed blood pressure **≥150/90 mmHg** (or >20/10 mmHg above target), prompt initiation of **two first-line antihypertensive agents of different classes** is recommended to achieve timely BP control. (Class I, Level A)
  * Preferred first-line combinations: **ACEi (or ARB) + Dihydropyridine CCB (Amlodipine)** OR **ACEi (or ARB) + Thiazide-like Diuretic (Chlorthalidone or Indapamide)**.
* **Recommendation 1.3 (Renal Protection in Albuminuria):** In patients with hypertension and urinary albumin-to-creatinine ratio (UACR) ≥30 mg/g, an **ACE inhibitor** (Lisinopril, Enalapril) or an **ARB** (Losartan, Valsartan) is the mandatory first-line therapy to slow progression of renal disease. (Class I, Level A)
  * *NEVER combine an ACE inhibitor with an ARB* due to increased adverse events (hyperkalemia, syncope, acute kidney injury) without added benefit (ONTARGET trial). (Class III: Harm)

### 2. Lipid Management & LDL Cholesterol Targets
* **Recommendation 2.1 (Secondary Prevention in Established ASCVD):** High-intensity statin therapy (**Atorvastatin 80mg** or **Rosuvastatin 40mg**) is recommended with a goal of achieving an LDL-C reduction of **≥50% from baseline** and an absolute **LDL-C target of <55 mg/dL (<1.4 mmol/L)**. (Class I, Level A)
* **Recommendation 2.2 (Add-on Lipid Therapies):** If LDL-C remains ≥55 mg/dL on maximally tolerated statin therapy:
  * Step 1: Add **Ezetimibe 10mg daily** (IMPROVE-IT trial, Class I, Level A).
  * Step 2: Add a **PCSK9 Inhibitor** (Evolocumab 140mg Q2W or Alirocumab 75–150mg Q2W) for very high-risk ASCVD patients with persistent LDL-C elevation (FOURIER & ODYSSEY OUTCOMES trials, Class I, Level A).
